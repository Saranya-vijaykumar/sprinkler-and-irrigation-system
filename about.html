Core Engineering Principles

The 4 Pillars of Sustainable Hydraulics

Every GreenWave system is engineered from the ground up to maximize soil percolation and eliminate evaporation.

Head-to-Head Matched Precipitation

Every spray head throws water to the base of the adjacent head. This guarantees uniform soil penetration with zero dry brown patches or overwatered mushy turf.

Doppler Satellite Radar Sync

Our WiFi controllers poll hyper-local National Weather Service radar every hour. If rain, high wind, or freezing frost is detected, your cycle automatically skips.

Trenchless Pipe-Pulling

We use specialized vibratory plows that pull polyethylene piping 10 inches underground without backhoe trenching — leaving your sod pristine.

5-Year Craftsmanship Guarantee

We stand behind every Rain Bird rotor, Hunter solenoid, brass backflow valve, and lateral pipe seam with a full 5-year parts and labor warranty.

Engineering Excellence

Anatomy of a Commercial-Grade Irrigation System

An effective sprinkler network is not just PVC pipes in dirt—it is an integrated hydraulic network engineered for balanced pressure, zero head misting, and intelligent root-zone delivery.

1. Certified Backflow Prevention

Every GreenWave system integrates a lead-free, brass RPZ (Reduced Pressure Zone) or double-check backflow preventer. Tested by certified BPAT inspectors to guarantee zero contaminated irrigation water enters your household drinking supply.

Meets strict TCEQ & municipal codes

2. Master Valve & Flow Sensor

Positioned immediately after the water meter, the normally-closed master solenoid valve only opens when an active cycle begins. Paired with ultrasonic flow meters, it automatically cuts water within 60 seconds if a pipe rupture occurs.

Prevents overnight yard sinkholes

3. Heavy-Duty Solenoid Manifolds

Housed in commercial jumbo valve boxes with crushed-gravel sub-drainage, our Rain Bird and Hunter jar-top valves feature captive solenoids and internal bleed screws for dependable decades-long multi-zone switching.

Waterproof silicon gel wire splices

4. Trenchless Virgin Poly Piping

We pull 100-PSI rated, NSF-certified high-density virgin polyethylene pipe 8-10 inches underground using vibratory plows. Flexible poly expands naturally during minor winter freezes without brittle shatter, ensuring 50+ year durability.

Zero sod scarring or trench settling

5. Pressure-Regulated Rotary Heads

Traditional heads turn water into useless aerosol mist when water pressure spikes above 50 PSI. GreenWave installs internal PRS (Pressure-Regulated Stem) heads locked at 40 PSI, generating thick wind-resistant droplets that land on grass.

Built-in Seal-A-Matic check valves

6. Satellite AI & Cloud Scheduling

The command center of your property. Our WiFi controllers connect directly to hyperlocal radar satellites, evaluating soil moisture deficit, wind speed, solar radiation, and rainfall predictions to dynamically water only when plants need it.

Instant iOS / Android smartphone management
The Science of Water

Why Amateur Sprinkler Layouts Fail & Waste Money

Most landscaping contractors install sprinklers by guessing head positions without calculating dynamic water pressure or precipitation rates. The result is uneven coverage, dry yellow circles, and thousands of gallons pouring onto roads.

GreenWave applies strict fluid dynamics. Every zone is designed with 100% matched head-to-head overlap—meaning every nozzle sprays directly to the base of its adjacent partner head, generating an even blanket of hydration across your turf.

Matched Precipitation: No puddling on slopes or dry spots in center.
Separate Microclimates: Shady turf and sunny lawns never share the same zone.
Cycle & Soak Technology: Prevents soil runoff by staggering brief soak bursts.
