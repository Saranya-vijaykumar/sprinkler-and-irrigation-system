Commercial Facility Challenge

Undetected Underground Mainline Ruptures & Surcharge Penalties

Commercial campuses often operate automated irrigation systems overnight between 1:00 AM and 5:00 AM. In this facility, an undetected 2-inch PVC mainline split under a parking lot island ran unchecked for weeks, discharging hundreds of thousands of gallons into storm drains and triggering excessive municipal peak-tier water rates.

Enterprise IoT Solution

Hydrawise Central Management & Instant Line-Burst Shutoff

GreenWave deployed industrial Toro glass-filled nylon valves and an ultrasonic flow monitoring network. If water flow deviates by more than 10% from baseline GPM, the central controller automatically isolates the zone, sends an SMS alert to facilities management, and dispatches a GreenWave commercial repair crew within 4 hours.
