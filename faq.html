Smart WiFi Controllers & Savings

Standard clock timers water blindly on a schedule regardless of rainfall. Smart controllers (such as Rachio 3 or Hunter Hydrawise) sync with live Doppler weather radar and hyper-local station data. If rain is forecast, the controller automatically skips the cycle. It also calculates solar radiation, humidity, and wind speed to adjust runtime down during cool periods.
Yes! All smart controllers we install include free iOS and Android mobile apps. You can monitor zone status, pause watering, run test cycles, or receive leak notifications anywhere in the world.

Winterization & Freeze Protection

Water expands by approximately 9% when freezing. Residual moisture trapped in subterranean lateral pipes, solenoid valves, and backflow assemblies will expand and crack the rigid PVC or brass bodies. Our commercial 185+ CFM compressor forces all moisture out of every zone, guaranteeing zero freeze damage.
All new turnkey installations carry our full 5-year warranty covering underground pipe joints, fittings, solenoid valves, wiring connections, and backflow preventers. If a subterranean leak or valve failure occurs under normal operation, we repair it with zero labor or parts charges.
