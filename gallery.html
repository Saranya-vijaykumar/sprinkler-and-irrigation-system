The Science Behind the Results

Why GreenWave Transformations Endure

Unlike quick-fix landscapers who merely replace sprinkler heads, every GreenWave installation adheres to 4 non-negotiable hydraulic engineering principles.

Dynamic 45 PSI Regulation

Eliminates high-pressure misting and fogging. Water droplets remain heavy and reach root crowns rather than evaporating in the wind.

Head-to-Head 100% Overlap

Every square inch of turf is covered by the overlapping spray radius of adjacent heads, eliminating brown circles and dry dead rings permanently.

Live Satellite Doppler Sync

Direct weather station connectivity automatically pauses scheduled watering cycles ahead of rain, storms, or sub-freezing cold snaps.

5-Year Craftsmanship Warranty

Every fitting, master valve, lateral pipe, and smart controller connection is 100% backed for parts and labor by Licensed Texas Irrigators.
