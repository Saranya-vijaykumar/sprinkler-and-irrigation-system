OUR TRENCHLESS PROTOCOL

Surgical Installation with Zero Lawn Scarring

We treat established estate turf like delicate carpet. Our modern vibratory insertion technology places pipe under roots without excavating open trenches.

01

CAD Topo & Pressure Audit

We map dynamic flow (GPM) and static PSI across every zone to eliminate friction loss and pressure drops before pipe is laid.

02

Vibratory Blade Insertion

Heavy-duty oscillating vibratory plows pull flexible poly laterals 10 inches below the turf, leaving sod perfectly intact.

04

Laser Flow Calibration

Every rotary nozzle is micro-adjusted to guarantee head-to-head coverage with crisp borders and zero overspray on masonry.
