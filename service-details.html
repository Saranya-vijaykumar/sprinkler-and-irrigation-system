Why Our Hydraulic Engineering Outperforms Traditional Installs

Most standard sprinkler installations suffer from unequal water pressure, causing the heads furthest from the valve to drizzle while the closest heads mist into useless evaporation clouds. At GreenWave, every installation is engineered with a strict dynamic pressure budget and calibrated head-to-head spray overlap.

We employ advanced vibratory plows that pull heavy-duty polyethylene lines directly beneath the turf surface, making a tiny incision that knits back together within 48 to 72 hours—leaving your lawn looking untouched.

Our 4-Phase Installation Methodology

01

Pressure & Soil Audit

We measure static & working GPM (Gallons Per Minute), soil compaction, sun exposure patterns, and flowerbed slopes.

02

Trenchless Pipe Pulling

Using vibratory pullers, lines are laid 8-10 inches deep without messy backhoe trenches that ruin sod or root systems.

03

Commercial Valves & Rotors

Heavy-duty Rain Bird or Hunter commercial manifold valves and check-valve popup heads prevent low-head drainage.

04

Cloud Sync & Walkthrough

We configure your smart app, link it to hyper-local weather satellites, test every zone, and hand over warranty documents.
