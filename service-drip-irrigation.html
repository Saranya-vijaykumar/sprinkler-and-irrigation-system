Micro-Irrigation Engineering

Why Subsurface Drip is the Gold Standard for Planting Beds

Spraying delicate flowering beds with high-pressure rotary spray heads is destructive and inefficient: over 40% of the water evaporates into the wind, soaking building foundations and causing powdery mildew on foliage.

GreenWave subsurface drip networks conceal heavy-duty pressure-compensating emitter tubing beneath mulch or topsoil. Every emitter releases a metered 0.4 to 0.9 GPH (gallons per hour) directly into root zones, achieving 95%+ water application efficiency.

Key Drip Irrigation Advantages

Copper Shield Technology

Embedded copper strip prevents root intrusion into emitter orifices without harmful chemical leach.

Pressure-Compensating Emitters

Ensures equal volume at the start, middle, and end of a 300-foot run on slopes and terraced gardens.

Completely Concealed Tubing

Installed cleanly beneath 2-3 inches of hardwood mulch or river rock, invisible to visitors.

Zero Leaf Blight & Rot

Keeps foliage dry while nourishing root zones, preventing bacterial leaf spot and fungal blight.

Engineering & System Specifications

Tubing Composition UV-resistant virgin polyethylene with dual-layer carbon black interior (50 PSI rating)
Emitter Spacing 12-inch or 18-inch calibrated inline emitters (0.4 GPH, 0.6 GPH, or 0.9 GPH)
Filtration Standard Stainless steel 200-mesh disc filter with integrated pressure regulator at valve manifold
Flush Valve Assembly Automatic subsurface flush valve at end-of-line to purge sediment on every cycle
