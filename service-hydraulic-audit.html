Discover Exactly Why Your Lawn Suffers & Where Water is Wasted

Most homeowners adjust runtimes arbitrarily, pouring thousands of gallons of excess water onto saturated slopes while wondering why sunny patches remain dry and brown. The root cause is almost always mismatched precipitation rates and incorrect operating pressure.

A GreenWave 12-Point Hydraulic Audit takes the guesswork out of yard care. Our licensed irrigators test dynamic operating PSI at every head, deploy calibrated catch cups to calculate exact distribution uniformity (DU), and deliver a clear written action plan.

The 12-Point Audit Checklist

Dynamic Operating PSI Test

Ensures rotors run at optimal 40-45 PSI and spray heads at 30 PSI to prevent atomized misting.

Catch-Cup Precipitation Test

Measures exact inches-per-hour water distribution across all turf areas to eliminate brown patches.

Head-to-Head Overlap Audit

Identifies spray shadow zones and improper arc patterns that starve grass of vital moisture.

Written ROI & Savings Plan

Itemized blueprint detailing nozzle upgrades, smart controller ROI, and city rebate eligibility.
