Fast Diagnosis & Upfront Repairs by Licensed Irrigators

A broken sprinkler head can pump out 12 to 20 gallons of water per minute, turning lawns into muddy sinkholes, killing grass roots, and driving utility water bills up by hundreds of dollars in a single weekend.

GreenWave repair trucks are rolling warehouses carrying over 400 OEM parts: Rain Bird, Hunter, Toro, and Irritrol. We use electronic wire trackers to locate buried valve boxes without digging random exploratory holes across your yard.
