Why Small Home Compressors Crack Valves & Ruin Pipes

When winter temperatures drop below 32°F, trapped water inside underground pipes expands by 9% with immense force, easily fracturing PVC pipe walls, shattering brass backflow bodies, and cracking solenoid valve manifolds.

Using a small homeowner garage compressor to blow out lines is dangerous: low air volume (CFM) forces operators to dial up pressure above 80 PSI, melting plastic gears and causing friction blowouts. GreenWave utilizes industrial 185+ CFM rotary-screw compressors operating at a gentle, safe 45-50 PSI—using high air volume, not destructive pressure, to sweep every line bone-dry.

Our Winterization Protocol

185+ CFM Industrial Volume

Commercial volume air evacuates all moisture from lateral lines in under 90 seconds per zone.

Strict 50 PSI Pressure Cap

Safeguards internal rotor gears, wiper seals, and nozzle springs against over-pressure damage.

Backflow Isolation & Drainage

Test cocks are drained and left at 45-degree angles to prevent brass freeze fractures.

100% Freeze Guarantee

If any properly winterized line or valve freeze-bursts over winter, we repair it 100% free of charge.
